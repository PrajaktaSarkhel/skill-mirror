💡 Reusable Practice Takeaway

The Active Prediction Inversion

Instead of asking AI to write or explain code, flip the dynamic: require the human (or yourself) to predict execution output and state transitions before inspecting results. The gap between your predicted model and reality is where genuine learning happens.
